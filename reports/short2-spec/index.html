Short ECPP v2: the radical-capped revision

2026-08-21 · AVS + Claude (Fable 5) · OneShotFastECPP, branch short-ecpp. Working note for the August 2026 revision of the ShortPrimalityProofs format; design discussion in the session of 2026-08-19–21 (see reports/ecpp-varieties/HANDOFF.md) and the write-up reports/ecpp-varieties/ecpp-varieties.pdf (Definition 6.2, Proposition 6.4).

The revision

A certificate is, as before, the flat sequence (p_0, A_0, x_0, o_0, A_1, x_1, o_1, ..., A_k, x_k, o_k) with n = ceil(log2 p_0) fixed for the whole chain, each level working modulo p_i with o_i = m_i · p_{i+1}. The revision changes three clauses, with B = ceil(n²/log₂ n):

  1. Smoothness. m_i is the B-smooth part of o_i (every prime of m_i is ≤ B; no prime of o_i may lie in (B, n²]).
  2. Radical cap. log₂ rad(m_i) ≤ ceil(n/log₂ n), where rad(m) is the product of the distinct primes of m (exact as a bit-length test: a squarefree power of 2 is just 2).
  3. Roughness and descent. p_1,…,p_{k+1} have no prime factor ≤ B (stated explicitly — for the recursive p_i it follows from the other conditions, for p_{k+1} it is load-bearing), with B² < p_{i+1} < √p_i for i < k and p_{k+1} < B²: a rough integer below B² is 1 or automatically prime, so the terminal prime certifies itself by size.

Window (L_i < o_i < r_i L_i, r_i the least prime of m_i), minimality, point-order semantics, and the payload are unchanged. A certificate with p_{k+1} = 1 is also valid under the original definition; one that uses a terminal prime is not — it is shorter than any chain the original format admits (the next section's truncation is forced, not optional). Existence for every prime p_0 ≥ 5 is untouched: a 2-power filler has radical 2 and p_{k+1} = 1.

Why: verification becomes O(n² log n), self-contained

The original format verifies in O(n²(log n)²) worst case (its k = 0 stratum is the unrestricted-filler restricted one-shot), and reaching O(n² log n) on published certificates needs the product tree of the primes to n² — a per-n table costing Θ(n²(log n)²) to build. The two caps remove both obstacles at once:

  • The primorial of B has Θ(n²/log n) bits, so it is built per certificate in O(n² log n) — no table.
  • gcd(P_B mod o_i, o_i) = rad(m_i) exactly, an integer of at most ceil(n/log₂ n) bits whose prime factors are ≤ B; the radicals of the whole chain are factored within the budget (batched against the primorial tree, or per-level Pollard–Strassen; the reference verifier simply trial-divides — coarsest asymptotically, simplest, and dominant only far beyond feasible sizes).
  • The radical cap bounds the order-exactness tree by (n/log₂ n)·ceil(log₂ k) < n ladder bits per level, so the elliptic work is O(Σ b_i M(b_i)) = O(n² log n), about 5× Pomerance's constant.

Proposition. A v2 certificate for an n-bit p_0 is verified in O(n² log n) bit operations and O(n²) bits of memory, worst case over certificates, with no precomputation of any kind.

The finding analysis is untouched: the caps constrain only window completion, a polynomial factor. 74% of the 120 published levels satisfied both caps incidentally (a per-level acceptance factor of ≈1.35 for a finder that filters).

Validation. Every prime in [5, 4096] (562 primes) was swept with short2.gp: all have v2 certificates, as the 2-power existence argument guarantees. The reference verifier is also faster than the (batched) original in practice: 5.6 vs 14.6 ms at 233 bits, 44 vs 141 ms at 665 bits, 174 vs 196 ms at 1030 bits (pure Python, this machine) — the primorial of B is a fraction of the n² sieve's work.

Terminal-prime revision (2026-08-22)

A second revision, proposed by Drew after the v2 merge: raise the recursion floor from n² to B² and let the terminal cofactor be a prime below B² — being B-rough, an integer below B² is 1 or automatically prime, so the last prime certifies itself by size (no factoring, no primality test, one extra exactness leaf). The recursion stops as soon as this is possible, and n² disappears from the format entirely. Key specification subtlety (Drew): B-roughness of pk+1 must be stated explicitly — for the recursive pi it follows from the other conditions (they are proven prime and exceed B²), but nothing else forces it for pk+1, and it is what makes the size certification sound; stated on the pi it also pins the smooth×rough decomposition of the stored products. (Scoped to p1,…,pk+1: applied to p0 it would exclude p0 = 5, where B = 5.)

Effects: 21 of the 31 chains shorten by exactly one level (129 → 108 levels, absorbed terminal primes of 15–32 bits) by pure truncation — no re-searching — and the raised floor makes the truncation forced (the old longer forms are invalid, which the verifier tests check). The 1010 chain becomes a single-level certificate. short8all.txt is provably unchanged: the exhaustive enumeration below 28 under the new rules coincides with the current file (equal counts + containment). short.gp gains the self-certifying terminal channel (a′) and loses an ispseudoprime call.
